01. Abstract

Euro Green (€GREEN) is an institutional-grade, RWA-backed ESG asset deployed on the Solana blockchain. Built on the Token-2022 standard, €GREEN introduces a native on-chain "Green Audit" mechanism. By leveraging Transfer Hooks, every transaction is tracked for its carbon footprint, ensuring full compliance with modern ESG standards.

Unlike traditional "green" tokens that rely on promises, €GREEN is backed by verified carbon credits and physical forest reserves, providing a hard price floor and tangible value.

04. Technology: Token-2022 & Green Audit

Standard SPL tokens on Solana are static. €GREEN utilizes Token-2022 with the Transfer Hook extension. This means we have a smart contract that intercepts every transfer to calculate the carbon footprint and automatically route a percentage of network fees to verified carbon offset registries.
